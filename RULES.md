# Starfall Gambit — Rules

**Goal:** destroy the enemy **Flagship**. You are **Blue** (bottom of the screen) and move first. The computer is **Red**.

The board is 6×6. On your turn, do exactly one thing: **move one ship** or **fire one Lancer**.
Tap a ship to see its options: green dots are moves, red rings are captures, and yellow crosshairs are Lancer shots. Tap one to do it.

## Your fleet (7 ships each)

| Ship | Count | Moves | Captures |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flagship (F)** | 1 | 1 square in any direction | By moving onto an enemy. **If it's destroyed, you lose.** |
| **Drone (D)** | 2 | L-shaped jump like a chess knight, leaping over ships | By landing on an enemy |
| **Lancer (L)** | 2 | 1 square in any direction | **Only by firing.** It can't capture by moving. |
| **Shield (S)** | 2 | 1 square up, down, left or right | By moving onto an enemy. **Immune to Lancer shots.** |

**Starting setup:** each back row is Lancer, Drone, Shield, Flagship, Drone, Lancer, with the second Shield one square in front of the Flagship.

### Lancer shots
- Instead of moving, a Lancer can fire **up, down, left or right**, up to **3 squares**.
- The shot hits the **first ship** in that line. If that ship is an enemy, it's destroyed. If it's your own ship or a Shield, the shot is blocked.
- Shots can't pass through nebula squares.
- The Lancer doesn't move when it fires.

## The nebula
- Every **3 rounds** (one round = your move plus Red's move), the nebula **pulses**: 4 squares in the middle four rows become nebula and the previous nebula clears.
- The pattern is always a mirror image (rotated 180°), so neither side is favored.
- The squares about to turn into nebula **flicker during the round before the pulse**, so you can see it coming.
- No ship can move into a nebula square, and Lancer shots can't pass through one.
- A ship already sitting on a square when it turns into nebula is **jammed**. It can't move or fire, but nothing can reach it either. It's freed when the nebula moves on.

## How the game ends
1. **Flagship destroyed:** the player who destroyed it wins.
2. **Stranded:** if a player has no legal move on their turn, they lose.
3. **Round limit:** after **40 rounds**, the side with more fleet points left wins (Lancer 3, Drone 3, Shield 2; the Flagship doesn't count). Equal points means a draw.

There's no "check" rule, so you're allowed to leave your Flagship in danger. A red flashing ring warns you when your Flagship is under fire.
