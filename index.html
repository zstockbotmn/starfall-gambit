Starfall Gambit

Your turn
Round 1/40 Nebula in 3
Tap one of your blue ships to begin.

How to play

Goal: destroy the enemy Flagship. The board is 6×6. You are Blue (bottom) and move first; the computer is Red (top).

On your turn, do one thing: move one ship or fire one Lancer. Tap a ship to see its options: green dots are moves, red rings are captures, and yellow crosshairs are Lancer shots. Tap one to do it. Red-striped squares show where enemy Lancers could hit.

Your fleet (7 ships)

  • F
    Flagship ×1: moves 1 square in any direction and captures by moving onto an enemy. If it's destroyed, you lose.
  • D
    Drone ×2: jumps in an L shape like a chess knight and captures where it lands. It can jump over ships and over nebula, but it can't land on nebula.
  • L
    Lancer ×2: moves 1 square in any direction but can't capture by moving. Instead it can fire up, down, left or right, up to 3 squares, and the Lancer doesn't move. The shot stops at the first ship in the line. If that ship is an enemy, it's destroyed. Your own ships block your shots, and so do Shields and nebula.
  • S
    Shield ×2: moves 1 square up, down, left or right and captures by moving. Lancer shots can't hurt a Shield, and Shields block shots, so park them in front of your Flagship.

Starting setup: each back row is Lancer, Drone, Shield, Flagship, Drone, Lancer, with the second Shield just in front of the Flagship. Red's setup is Blue's mirrored top-to-bottom.

The nebula

  • About every 3 rounds the nebula shifts. The old nebula fully clears, and 4 new squares in the middle four rows (rows 2–5) become nebula. They never overlap the squares that just cleared.
  • The pattern is mirrored top-to-bottom, just like the starting setup: a nebula square in row 2 has a twin in the same column in row 5, and row 3 pairs with row 4. Neither side is favored.
  • Shifts alternate between happening after Red's move and after yours, so each side takes turns being first to react.
  • The squares about to become nebula flicker one round ahead.
  • No ship can move into nebula, and shots can't pass through it.
  • A shift can land on squares that already have ships, and you're allowed to move onto a flickering square. Any ship on a square when it becomes nebula is jammed: it can't move or fire, but nothing can reach it. It's free again when the nebula shifts.

Game end

  • Destroy the enemy Flagship and you win.
  • No legal move loses: if it's your turn and none of your ships can move or fire (for example, all blocked or jammed by the nebula), you're stranded and you lose.
  • After 40 rounds, the fleet with more points left wins: Lancer 3, Drone 3, Shield 2. The Flagship isn't counted. Equal points means a draw.

There's no "check" rule, so you're allowed to leave your Flagship in danger. A red flashing ring warns you when it's under fire.
