How to play

Goal: destroy the enemy Flagship. You are Blue (bottom) and move first; the computer is Red.

On your turn, do one thing: move one ship, or fire one Lancer. Tap a ship to see its options: green dots are moves, red rings are captures, yellow crosshairs are Lancer shots. Tap one to do it.

Your fleet (7 ships)

  • F
    Flagship ×1 — moves 1 square in any direction and captures by moving onto an enemy. If it's destroyed, you lose.
  • D
    Drone ×2 — jumps in an L shape like a chess knight (it can leap over ships) and captures where it lands.
  • L
    Lancer ×2 — moves 1 square in any direction but can't capture by moving. Instead it can fire: it destroys the first enemy in a straight line (up, down, left, right) up to 3 squares away. Any ship in between blocks the shot. The Lancer stays where it is.
  • S
    Shield ×2 — moves 1 square up, down, left or right and captures by moving. Lancer shots can't hurt a Shield, and Shields block shots, so park them in front of your Flagship.

The nebula

Every 3 rounds the nebula pulses: 4 squares in the middle of the board turn into nebula (always in a mirror-image pattern, so it's fair), and the old nebula clears. The squares that are about to change flicker one round ahead as a warning.

  • No ship can move into a nebula square, and shots can't pass through it.
  • A ship caught inside is jammed until the nebula moves on. It can't move or fire, but nothing can reach it either.

Game end

  • Destroy the enemy Flagship and you win.
  • If a player has no legal move on their turn, they're stranded and lose.
  • After 40 rounds the fleet with more points left wins (Lancer 3, Drone 3, Shield 2). Equal points means a draw.

You're allowed to leave your Flagship in danger, but the computer will notice. A red ring warns you when your Flagship is under fire.
